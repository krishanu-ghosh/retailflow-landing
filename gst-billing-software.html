GST billing software FAQ

What is GST billing software?

GST billing software creates bills that follow India’s GST rules. It applies the right GST rate to each item, splits the tax into CGST and SGST for sales within your state or IGST for sales to another state, and prints a GST invoice with your GSTIN. RetailFlow does this on every bill automatically.

Is RetailFlow’s GST billing software free?

Yes, right now. RetailFlow is free during its Early Access Program, with every module unlocked and no card needed. When Early Access ends, plans start from ₹149 a month, and Early Access customers get at least 50% off for a minimum of 12 months. See pricing.

Does it handle both CGST + SGST and IGST?

Yes. You choose intra-state (CGST + SGST) or inter-state (IGST) on the payment screen, and the tax on the invoice is split accordingly.

Can I bill at MRP with GST included?

Yes. Each product can be priced GST-inclusive (MRP) or GST-exclusive. RetailFlow works out the taxable value and the tax either way, and shows the price the customer will pay while you set it.

Does RetailFlow support GST e-invoicing?

Yes. RetailFlow generates e-invoices (IRN), validates customer GSTINs, and handles TDS and TCS.

What does my CA get from the GST software?

GST summaries, profit and loss, and a balance sheet built from the same sales records as your bills. If your CA works in Tally, you can export the accounting data for the period instead of re-entering it.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. RetailFlow runs in the browser on a computer or phone, with a mobile layout for billing and stock checks, and you can scan barcodes with your phone camera. An Android app is coming soon.
