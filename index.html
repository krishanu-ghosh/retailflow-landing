// questions

GST billing, answered.

Q. What is GST billing software?

GST billing software calculates the correct tax on every item — CGST/SGST or IGST, depending on the sale — and generates a compliant invoice automatically. RetailFlow does this on every bill: set a GST rate once per product, and every sale is tax-correct without manual calculation.

Q. Is RetailFlow billing software for small shops, or only larger stores?

Both. RetailFlow is billing software for retail businesses of any size — from a single counter to a store with multiple staff and access levels. Keyboard-first checkout keeps a solo counter fast, while role-based access covers bigger teams.

Q. Does RetailFlow generate GST-compliant invoices automatically?

Yes. Every invoice is GST-compliant by default — tax is calculated per product, printed on receipts or sent over WhatsApp, and ready for filing without exporting anything to Excel.

Q. What's the difference between GST billing and regular billing software?

Regular billing software just prints a receipt. GST billing software calculates tax correctly per item and produces an invoice that's legally compliant for GST-registered businesses. RetailFlow does this by default on every bill, not as an add-on.

Q. Do I need separate accounting software alongside RetailFlow's billing?

No. RetailFlow's billing, GST calculation, cashbook, and accounting all run off the same sales data, so your books stay in sync automatically — no re-entry, no separate export step.

Q. Can RetailFlow handle more than one store location?

Yes. Assign staff to specific locations, track inventory separately per store, and transfer stock between them — all from one login. You can also publish a branded online storefront alongside your physical counters, with every order drawing from the same stock and billing system.
