// questions

Pricing FAQ

Q. Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial when you sign up — pick a tier and start billing immediately, no credit card required to begin.

Q. What happens when my trial or subscription ends?

Access pauses until a payment is recorded for your plan — your data is never deleted, just paused. Reach out before your trial ends and we'll get your subscription set up.

Q. Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move up or down between tiers as your business changes — your sales, inventory, and customer data stay exactly as they are either way.

Q. Do these prices include GST?

No. Prices shown are exclusive of GST — 18% GST is added on your invoice, same as any GST-registered service in India.

Q. What if I'm not sure which plan fits, or need something beyond Enterprise?

Reach out through the contact form below — tell us about your business and we'll help you pick the right tier, or put together something custom.

Q. What happened to Early Access?

If you joined RetailFlow during Early Access, nothing changes for you — reach out via the contact form if you have questions about your account.
