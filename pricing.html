// questions

Pricing FAQ

Q. Is RetailFlow really free right now?

Yes. Every module is unlocked during Early Access — POS billing, inventory, GST invoicing, reports, and multi-user roles. No credit card required to sign up.

Q. Will RetailFlow always be free?

We haven't finalized paid pricing yet. Once we do, Early Access members get advance notice and won't be moved to a paid plan without being told first.

Q. What discount do Early Access members get?

When we introduce paid pricing, everyone who joined during Early Access gets at least 50% off for a minimum of 12 months — locked in, no separate sign-up needed.

Q. Will I lose my data when paid plans launch?

No. Your sales, inventory, customer, and invoice data stays exactly as it is. Pricing changes affect access going forward, not the data you've already built.

Q. Do I need a credit card to join Early Access?

No credit card, no trial countdown, and no sales call. Open RetailFlow Web and start billing.
