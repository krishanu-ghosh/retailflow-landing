Pricing FAQ

Is there a free trial?

Right now you don’t need one, RetailFlow is free during EAP. Once standard pricing applies, every tier still starts with a 7-day free trial, pick a plan and start billing immediately, no card needed to begin.

What happens when my trial or subscription ends?

Access pauses until a payment is recorded for your plan, your data is never deleted, just paused. Reach out before your trial ends and we’ll get your subscription set up.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Move up or down between tiers as your business changes, your sales, inventory, and customer data stay exactly as they are either way.

Do these prices include GST?

No. Prices shown are exclusive of GST, 18% GST is added on your invoice, same as any GST-registered service in India.

What if I’m not sure which plan fits, or need something beyond Enterprise?

Reach out through the contact form below, tell us about your business and we’ll help you pick the right tier, or put together something custom.

What is the Early Access Program (EAP), and what happens when it ends?

RetailFlow is completely free right now during EAP, every module unlocked, no card needed. The tiers above are the standard pricing that takes effect once EAP ends. EAP customers get at least 50% off any tier for a minimum of 12 months, applied automatically.
